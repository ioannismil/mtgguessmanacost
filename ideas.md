# Project Ideas

## Gameplay Enhancements
- **Hint System**: Allow users to spend score/streak to get a hint (e.g., reveal 1 color, reveal CMC).
- **Power/Toughness Higher or Lower**: A variation of the Higher/Lower game using creature stats.
- **"Sudden Death" Mode**: A game mode with 1 life and increasing difficulty.
- **Set/Era Filters**: Allow filtering by specific blocks or time periods (e.g., "Pre-Modern", "Recent Sets").

## UI/UX Improvements
- **Card Flip Animation**: Add a 3D flip effect when revealing the card or the answer.
- **Dynamic Backgrounds**: Change the page background color subtly based on the current card's color identity.
- **Sound Effects**: Add optional sound effects for correct/incorrect guesses and game over.
- **Confetti Celebration**: Trigger a confetti explosion for high streaks or high scores.

## Social & Retention
- **Share Result**: Add a "Share" button to copy the score/streak to clipboard for easy sharing on social media.
- **Daily Challenge**: A fixed seed of 5 cards per day so everyone guesses the same ones; leaderboard for the daily challenge.
